Rut Score is a conditions guide, not a guarantee. The Rut Score rates how active the rut is; the Hunt Score weighs the temperature change, barometric pressure trend, wind, rain, sunrise and sunset, the rut, solunar periods and moon phase. Always confirm legal shooting hours and seasons with your state wildlife agency, wear blaze orange where required, and use a safety harness in tree stands.

Which stand should I hunt?

Deer smell you long before they see you. The right stand is the one where today's wind carries your scent away from where deer come from.

  1. Pin each stand on the satellite map.
  2. Point the arrow toward where deer show up when you sit there: a trail, field edge or creek crossing. Or stand in it and use Set from my phone.
  3. Rut Score checks the wind at prime time and tells you which stand to hunt.
